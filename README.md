# Walmart Sales Performance Analysis (Tableau)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes Walmart sales data to understand sales performance, store segmentation, and the impact of holidays and economic indicators on revenue.

Two interactive Tableau dashboards were created:
1. Executive Overview
2. Store Performance, Trends & Economic Context

The goal is to present business-ready insights using KPIs, trends, comparisons, and filters.

---

## 📊 Dashboards Included

### 1️⃣ Executive Overview Dashboard
**Key KPIs**
- Total Sales
- Average Weekly Sales
- Holiday Sales
- Holiday Sales Contribution %

**Visuals**
- Total Sales by Store Tier
- Weekly Sales Trend
- Holiday Impact by Store Tier

**Filters**
- Year
- Store Tier
- Sales Period (Holiday / Non-Holiday)

---

### 2️⃣ Store Performance & Trends Dashboard
**Visuals**
- Top 10 Stores by Total Sales
- Year-over-Year Sales Trend
- Average Weekly Sales vs Unemployment
- Holiday vs Non-Holiday Sales Comparison

**Filters**
- Year
- Store Tier
- Sales Period

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- Top-tier stores consistently outperform low-tier stores in both total and average weekly sales.
- Holiday periods contribute a significant uplift in sales, especially for top-tier stores.
- Sales peaked in 2011 and declined slightly in 2012.
- Average weekly sales show an inverse trend with unemployment over time.
